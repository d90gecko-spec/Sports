The Chase Web Application: README Welcome to The Chase (Sweet Edition)—a projection-friendly, high-contrast interactive web application built for live hosting, classroom engagement, and team game sessions. This guide outlines how the application works, the core game mechanics, and how to format and customize your question bank.

    Overview & Key Features
  • Customizable Title: The header includes an editable title box (defaulting to Sweet Edition) so you can rename your game on the fly.
  • Persistent Chaser Ladder: A 7-step board on the right side of the screen featuring clear visual markers for both the Player and the Chaser.
  • Interactive Cash Builder: A built-in 1-minute (60-second) rapid-fire counter where hosts can tally correct answers (worth 1 Sweet each). The timer automatically pulses and enlarges during the final 15 seconds to build anticipation!
  • Dynamic Sweet Offers: Automatically calculates offers based on the banked Cash Builder:
  • High Offer: 2× Cash Builder (Starts at Step 1, closer to the Chaser)
  • Middle Offer: Standard Cash Builder (Starts at Step 2)
  • Low Offer: 1/2 Cash Builder (Starts at Step 3, safer distance)
  • LaTeX Math Support: Fully integrated with KaTeX to render mathematical expressions in questions and options.

Game Flow & Host Instructions Phase 1: Setup & Cash Builder The Cash Builder Round: Click ⏱️ Play Cash Builder to open the 1-minute timer. As contestants shout out correct answers, click ✅ Correct to bank sweets (1 sweet per click). When time expires, click Bank Sweets & Return to Setup. Alternative Manual Entry: You can also directly type your total banked sweets into the Edit Cash Builder input box. Choose Your Offer: Review the High, Middle, and Low offers displayed on the board cards. Click your preferred offer to lock in your starting position on the 7-step ladder, then click Lock In Offer & Face The Chaser. Phase 2: Head-to-Head The Chase Answering Questions: Read the question and choose from the 3 multiple-choice options (A, B, or C). Correct Answer: The player moves forward 1 step up the ladder. Incorrect Answer: The player holds their position. The Chaser’s Turn: After each question round, use the Chaser Control Panel at the bottom of the question card: Click Yes (+1 Chaser Step) if the Chaser answered correctly. Click No (Hold Position) if the Chaser missed it. Win / Loss Conditions: Reach Step 6 (Home) to successfully outrun the Chaser and win your banked sweets! If the Chaser's position catches up to or overtakes the player's position, the game ends in a catch. Smart Reset: Clicking 🔄 Reset (Next Unanswered) automatically advances to the next question set in your bank so you never loop back to the same first question. Question Bank Formatting Guide You can fully customize, add, or replace questions via the ⚙️ Settings & Questions modal in the top-right corner. The question bank must be formatted as a valid JSON array of objects. Question Object Structure Each question item in the array requires three properties: q (string): The question text. Supports plain text and LaTeX math. options (array of 3 strings): Exactly three multiple-choice options. correct (integer): The zero-based index of the correct option (0 for the first option, 1 for the second, 2 for the third). Example JSON Template JSON [ { "q": "What is the value of $\\frac{10}{2}$?", "options": ["3", "5", "7"], "correct": 1 }, { "q": "Which planet is known as the Red Planet?", "options": ["Venus", "Mars", "Jupiter"], "correct": 1 } ] Using LaTeX Math Formatting To include mathematical notation in your questions or answer options, wrap your LaTeX syntax in single dollar signs ($...$) for inline math or double dollar signs ($$...$$) for block math. Example: What is $\\int_{0}^{1} x \, dx$?
