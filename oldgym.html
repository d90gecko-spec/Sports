8-Month Periodization Roadmap

Progression leading up to Outlaw Triathlon July 2027.

Winter Base (Nov - Jan)

Build unilateral stability, posterior chain resilience, and upper back posture. 3 strength sessions/week.

Spring Build (Feb - Apr)

Transition to 2 strength sessions/week. Focus on force transfer and knee/ankle durability for long runs.

Specialty & Peak (May - Jun)

Maintenance mode (1-2 short sessions/week). Prioritize race-pace specificity, taper freshness, and recovery.

Race Month (July 2027)

Outlaw Race execution. Zero heavy lifting in race week; mobility & activation only.
